# DFIR Tools & Technologies

A collection of digital forensics and incident response tools I have used during practical labs, investigations and cybersecurity training.

---

## Digital Forensics

- **Autopsy** – Digital forensic investigation and evidence analysis, including LLM-assisted workflows.
- **FTK Imager** – Forensic imaging and examination of digital storage media.
- **DB Browser for SQLite** – Examination and analysis of SQLite databases.

---

## SIEM & Endpoint Detection

- **CrowdStrike Falcon** – SIEM and EDR investigation, endpoint visibility and threat detection.
- **Wazuh** – Security monitoring, log analysis, alert investigation and threat detection.

---

## Network Analysis

- **Wireshark** – Network traffic capture and packet analysis.

---

## Operating Systems & Lab Environments

- **Kali Linux** – Security testing and investigation utilities.
- **Linux** – Command-line navigation, system administration and investigation workflows.
- **Virtual Machines** – Building and managing isolated lab environments for cybersecurity exercises.
- **SANS SIFT Workstation** – Digital forensic and incident response analysis environment.
